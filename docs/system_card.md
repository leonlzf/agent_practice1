# System Card

## Intended use

Assist authorized internal users with synthetic banking policy research and evidence retrieval.

## Prohibited use

- Final credit, eligibility, compliance, or legal decisions.
- Access to real customer or confidential bank data in this practice project.
- Tool execution outside the explicit allowlist.

## Current status

Scaffold only. All query requests safely escalate until retrieval, authorization, generation,
and verification are implemented and validated.

## Users and roles

The MVP records a declared analyst, reviewer, or validator role for scenario testing. A real
authentication and authorization system is out of scope.

## Human oversight

High-risk or unsupported requests must be escalated to a qualified human reviewer.
